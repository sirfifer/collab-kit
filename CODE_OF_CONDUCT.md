# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on Collab Kit. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull
requests, and reviews, is expected to make it a place others can work in too.

## What is expected

- Be direct about the work and respectful of the person. Disagree with what was written, not with
  who wrote it.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when something is wrong.
- Welcome people who are new, to the project or to the subject.
- Accept a correction, and give one the way you would want to get it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks, about anyone and for any reason, including who they are,
  where they are from, how they speak, or how much they know.
- Sexual language or attention that is not wanted.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Continuing after you have been asked to stop.

## What happens

The maintainer (one person today) may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the
project, for anything that breaks this code. After a first, minor lapse the person gets a private word. After a
serious or repeated one, the comment is removed or the person blocked, without one.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's own "Report content", in the menu on the comment in question: it always exists, and
it reaches GitHub. To reach the maintainer privately, use the contact on their GitHub profile if one
is shown. Reports are kept in confidence. Do not use a public issue for this.
