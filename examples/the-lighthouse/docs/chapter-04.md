# Chapter four: the night the light went out (draft notes)

## The storm

It comes in from the north-west after dark. Maren is in the kitchen when the beam stops sweeping
the window. Elias takes the storm lantern and goes up.

## Where Tobias was

Tobias is three days out with the herring fleet, off the Dogger. He hears of his father's death
from the harbourmaster when the fleet comes in a week later.

## Morning

Maren finds the lamp still lit but the lens still: the clockwork has jammed. Her father is on the
rocks below the gallery. The logbook is not on the desk where he always left it.
