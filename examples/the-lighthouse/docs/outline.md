# The Lighthouse: outline

A novel in eight chapters. Skerry Point, a rock with a lighthouse and four houses, the winter the
light failed.

## The chapters

1. **The lamp.** Maren, nineteen, trims the wick for her father, the keeper Elias. The new
   clockwork that turns the lens has started to stick.
2. **The fleet.** Her brother Tobias leaves for the mainland with the herring fleet. Before he goes
   he spends a night in the lamp room with the clockwork.
3. **The glass.** The barometer falls for two days. Elias refuses to send for the engineer.
4. **The night the light went out.** The storm. The light stops turning. Elias goes up the tower
   and does not come down; he is found on the rocks at dawn.
5. **The inquiry.** The harbour board blames the keeper. Maren is asked to leave the Point.
6. **The logbook.** Tobias comes home. He has Elias's logbook, and the last page is torn out.
7. **The winter.** Maren keeps the light alone, unpaid, against the board's order.
8. **The relief.** The new keeper arrives. Maren gives him the light, and Tobias gives her the page.
