## Answer

The draft says two things. Chapter four: "three days out with the herring fleet, off the Dogger."
Chapter six: he comes home with the logbook, last page torn out. The logbook was missing from the
desk on the morning after the storm (chapter four, "Morning"). One of the two has to change, and
which one depends on whether Tobias knew the clockwork would fail (W-001).
