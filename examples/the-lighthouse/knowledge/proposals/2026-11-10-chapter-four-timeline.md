# Proposal: put Tobias on the island in chapter four

**Status:** waiting for the author (W-003)
**From:** C01, and the response of 2026-11-10

## What

Change chapter four's "Where Tobias was" (CH#where-tobias-was) from "three days out with the herring
fleet" to: he missed the fleet's sailing, told no one, and was sleeping in the boathouse.

## Why

Chapter six has him holding the logbook that disappeared from the lamp room that night (CH#morning).
With him at sea, nothing explains it.

## Suggested wording

"Tobias was not with the fleet. He had watched it sail from the boathouse roof, and had not gone
down to the harbour."
