Topic: Tobias cannot be at sea in chapter four
What was worked out: if Tobias has the logbook in chapter six, he was in the lamp room on the night of the storm, or someone gave it to him. Chapter four says he was with the fleet. The author thinks he was home, hiding, because he knew the clockwork would fail and came back to fix it, and was too late.
