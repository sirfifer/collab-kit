## Definition

Twenty-three. Would rather be at sea. Spent a night with the clockwork before he left (chapter two).

## Why it matters

The question the book turns on: did he know the clockwork would fail? Chapter four puts him three
days out with the fleet, but chapter six has him holding the logbook, which went missing from the
lamp room that same night. Both cannot be true (C01, W-001).
